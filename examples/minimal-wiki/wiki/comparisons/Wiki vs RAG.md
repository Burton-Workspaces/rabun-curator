# Wiki vs RAG

| | [[RAG]] | [[Persistent Wiki]] |
|---|---|---|
| When knowledge is assembled | Query time | Ingest time |
| What compounds | Usually nothing | Pages, links, flagged contradictions |
| Human role | Prompt + retrieve | Curate sources and questions |
| LLM role | Answer from retrieved chunks | Maintain the wiki |

Pattern: [[LLM Wiki]] ([[Andrej Karpathy]]).
