# Persistent Wiki

A structured, interlinked collection of markdown files the LLM writes and maintains. It sits between the human and immutable `raw/` sources.

Cross-references, contradiction flags, and synthesis live here so they are not re-derived on every question. Contrast with [[RAG]]. Parent pattern: [[LLM Wiki]].
