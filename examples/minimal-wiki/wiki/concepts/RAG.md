# RAG

Retrieval-augmented generation: chunk raw documents, retrieve at query time, generate. Works, but does not accumulate. Subtle questions that need five documents force rediscovery every time.

The [[LLM Wiki]] pattern compiles those connections into a [[Persistent Wiki]] instead. See [[Wiki vs RAG]].
